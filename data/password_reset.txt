Password Reset and Account Recovery

Purpose

Use this procedure when a user forgets a password, receives an invalid-password message, or cannot sign in after changing a password.

Self-Service Password Reset

1. Open the company password-reset portal.
2. Enter the company email address.
3. Select a verification method.
4. Enter the verification code.
5. Create a new password that meets the security requirements.
6. Sign in again using the new password.

The password must contain at least 12 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. The new password cannot match any of the previous five passwords.

Account Lockout

An account is temporarily locked after five unsuccessful sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes before trying again. Repeated attempts during the lockout period may restart the timer.

If the account remains locked after 15 minutes, contact the service desk. The service-desk technician must verify the user’s identity before unlocking the account.

Password Changed but Sign-In Still Fails

Restart the computer and reconnect it to the company network. If the user is working remotely, connect to the VPN using the updated password. Update saved passwords in email applications, mobile devices, and mapped network drives.

Escalation

Escalate the incident to Identity and Access Management when:

- The verification code is not received.
- The account repeatedly locks after a successful reset.
- The user no longer has access to any registered verification method.
- The password-reset portal displays a server error.

Never request, record, or send a user’s password through email, chat, or a support ticket.
