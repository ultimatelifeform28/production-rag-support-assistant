Email Synchronization Troubleshooting

Purpose

Use this procedure when new email messages do not appear, messages remain in the outbox, or calendar and contact changes do not synchronize.

Check Connectivity

Confirm that the device has a working internet connection. If the user is accessing email through a company network resource, connect to the VPN before continuing.

Check Service Status

Review the company service-status page for an active email outage. If an outage is confirmed, inform the user and document the incident. Do not remove or recreate the account during a known outage.

Restart Synchronization

1. Close the email application.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Reopen the application.
4. Select Send and Receive or Sync.
5. Allow several minutes for large mailboxes to update.

Verify Account Credentials

If the user recently changed a password, update the saved password in the email application. Complete multifactor authentication if prompted.

Never ask the user to provide a password through email, chat, or a support ticket.

Check Storage

Email may stop synchronizing when the mailbox or device storage is full. Delete unnecessary messages or local files only with the user’s approval. Emptying the deleted-items folder may recover mailbox space.

Common Problems

Messages Stuck in the Outbox:
Check for large attachments and confirm the recipient address. Remove the attachment and retry when it exceeds the company size limit.

New Messages Are Missing:
Check the junk-mail folder, inbox rules, filters, and focused-inbox settings.

Calendar Is Not Updating:
Confirm that the correct calendar is selected and that the application is online. Manually synchronize the calendar.

Mobile Device Is Not Synchronizing:
Confirm that background data and automatic synchronization are enabled. Restart the device before removing the account.

Escalation

Contact the service desk when:

- Synchronization fails on multiple devices.
- The application repeatedly requests a password.
- Mailbox permissions appear incorrect.
- The user receives a certificate or server error.
- The problem continues after restarting and manually synchronizing.

Record the affected device, email application, error message, time of failure, and troubleshooting steps in the support ticket.
