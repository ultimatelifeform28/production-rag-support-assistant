VPN Connection Troubleshooting

Purpose

Use this procedure when a remote user cannot connect to the company VPN or repeatedly loses the connection.

Check the Internet Connection

Before troubleshooting the VPN, confirm that the computer has a working internet connection. Open a trusted website without using the VPN. If the website does not load, restart the modem or router and contact the internet service provider if necessary.

Verify the VPN Sign-In

Open the approved company VPN application and confirm that the correct server is selected. Enter the company username and current password. Complete multifactor authentication when prompted.

If the user recently changed a password, remove any saved credentials from the VPN application and enter the updated password.

Restart the Connection

1. Disconnect from the VPN.
2. Close the VPN application.
3. Restart the computer.
4. Reopen the VPN application.
5. Attempt the connection again.

Common VPN Errors

Authentication Failed:
Confirm the username, password, and multifactor authentication code. The account may be locked after repeated unsuccessful attempts.

Connection Timed Out:
Check the internet connection and temporarily move closer to the wireless router. Public networks may block VPN traffic.

No Available VPN Server:
Wait five minutes and try again. Select the secondary company VPN server if it is available.

Connected but Company Resources Are Unavailable:
Disconnect and reconnect to the VPN. Confirm that the computer received a company network address. Restart the application or mapped drive that cannot reach the company resource.

Security Requirements

Do not disable antivirus software, the firewall, or device encryption to establish a VPN connection. Never use an unapproved VPN application.

Escalation

Contact the service desk when:

- The VPN account remains locked.
- Multifactor authentication does not work.
- Both company VPN servers are unavailable.
- The connection repeatedly drops on a stable internet connection.
- The VPN reports a certificate or security error.

Record the error message, time of failure, VPN server, and troubleshooting steps in the support ticket.
